# Acknowledgements

MQL5 CodeGraph is an independent open-source project shaped by the work and tools of a wider community.

## Project community

The project is maintained by [junet03](https://github.com/junet03). GitHub's
[**Contributors**](https://github.com/junet03/mql5-codegraph/graphs/contributors) page is the canonical,
history-derived record of people who have contributed commits. Issues, reviews, documentation, testing,
and community support are also valued even when they do not create a commit entry.

## Graphify Labs

Special thanks to [Safi Shamsi](https://github.com/safishamsi), Graphify Labs, and the
[Graphify contributors](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify/graphs/contributors) for building and
sharing [Graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify). Its approach to queryable,
provenance-aware project graphs inspired the optional semantic-navigation adapter in this project.
Graphify is an external Apache-2.0 project; its source is not vendored here.

## OpenAI Codex

Special thanks also to [OpenAI Codex](https://openai.com/codex/) and
[OpenAI](https://openai.com/). A substantial part of the implementation, tests, specifications, and
documentation has been developed through an agent-assisted “vibe coding” workflow with Codex. Project
maintainers direct, review, test, and remain responsible for the resulting work.
